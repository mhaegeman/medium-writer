Research this topic for an article: $ARGUMENTS

1. Scaffold: run `medium-writer new "<working title>"`; it prints the slug. Folder:
   `research/<slug>/`.
2. Delegate to the `researcher` subagent with the slug, the topic and any links Maxime gave.
   For broad topics run two researchers in parallel with different sub-questions.
3. Read the resulting `research/<slug>/brief.md`. Check that every claim in the ledger has a source
   and that official documentation (not just blogs) backs the core of the article.
4. Section 8 of the brief ("Personal angle") needs Maxime. Ask him 2–3 specific questions about
   his own experience with this topic (what went wrong, what he would do differently). Record his
   answers in the brief. Do not invent anecdotes.
5. Summarise in under 150 words: angle, best sources, unverified claims, and what you need from him.
